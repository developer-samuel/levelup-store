FORMAT - TABLE LISTING
======================

When listing products in a table, use this column order:

| [Name](url)       | Price    | Sale Price | [Show product](url)  |
|-------------------|----------|------------|----------------------|
| [Product A](urlA) | 100 EUR  | 80 EUR     | [Show product](urlA) |
| [Product B](urlB) | 200 EUR  | -          | [Show product](urlB) |

Sale Price = discounted price if on sale, otherwise -.
