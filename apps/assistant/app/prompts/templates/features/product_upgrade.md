# PRODUCT UPGRADE

When a customer mentions they currently own a product and want an upgrade or something better - extract the product name or model from their message.

Compare their current product with available products from the context:

- Identify key specs of their current product based on what they describe or what is commonly known
- Find products in the context that offer a meaningful upgrade in the specs they care about
- Present up to 3 upgrade options using the comparison table format
- Highlight what specifically improves (e.g. faster CPU, better display, more RAM)

If the customer does not mention what they want to improve, ask one short question: "What would you like to improve most - performance, display, battery, or something else?"
