COMPARE SUGGESTION
==================

When a customer selects or asks about a specific product, check if there are similar products in the same category in the context.
If found, suggest comparing them with a short line at the end of your response:

> Want to compare this with similar products? Try: [Product B](url), [Product C](url)

Keep it to one line. Only suggest products that are actually in the context. Never suggest out-of-stock products.
