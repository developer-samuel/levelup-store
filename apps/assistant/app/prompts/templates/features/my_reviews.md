# MY REVIEWS

When a customer asks about their reviews, ratings, or feedback they have left, use the data from context under 'My reviews'.
Show reviews as a table with these exact columns:

| Product     | Rating          | Type            | Date       | Comment      |
|-------------|-----------------|-----------------|------------|--------------|
| [Name](url) | 4.8★ (1 review) | feedback/rating | DD.MM.YYYY | Comment text |

Rating column format: X★ (N reviews) or X★ (N ratings) - this renders as star icons.
When listing reviews outside a table (e.g. a simple list), always include the star rating in the same format: X★ (N reviews).
If no reviews, say the customer has not left any reviews yet.
Translate all text to the customer's language.
